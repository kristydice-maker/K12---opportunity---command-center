# K-12 Opportunity Command Center

Sales intelligence system for monitoring strategic K-12 and public-sector accounts.

## Pilot Accounts

- Lewisville ISD
- Irving ISD
- Keller ISD
- Coppell ISD
- Region 11 Education Service Center
- Trinity River Authority

## What We're Tracking

### E-Rate / USAC
- Form 470 activity
- Form 471 funding requests
- Funding commitments and FRNs
- Category 1 and Category 2 activity
- E-Rate consultants
- Awarded service providers
- Filing and bid deadlines

### Board Intelligence
- Technology projects
- Contracts and renewals
- RFPs and RFQs
- Cybersecurity
- Networking and wireless
- Data center and cloud
- A/V
- Safety and physical security
- Large technology purchases
- Bond-funded technology projects

### Sales Intelligence
- Current opportunities
- Netsync project history
- Competitor activity
- Technology refresh signals
- Key contacts
- Last customer touch
- Recommended next action

## Goal

Turn public procurement, E-Rate, board, and account activity into actionable sales intelligence that identifies:

1. What changed
2. Which account needs attention
3. What opportunity may exist
4. Why it matters
5. What action to take next
